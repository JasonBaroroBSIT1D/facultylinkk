Features

Everything the assessment needs, in one place.

From supporting documents to reviewer verification, FacultyLink keeps each step of the evaluation organized.

  • 01

    Academic Document Management

    Organize evidence by KRA, criterion, and document type for self-assessment.

  • 02

    OCR-Assisted Document Extraction

    Simulated text extraction to preview fields for validation—not proof of authenticity.

  • 03

    Rule-Based Document Validation

    Automated checks against configured requirements, separate from human verification.

  • 04

    KRA Self-Assessment

    Track Instruction, Research, Extension, and Professional Development progress.

  • 05

    Reviewer Verification

    Reviewers approve, reject, or request revisions with remarks and verified points.

  • 06

    Academic Rank Simulation

    Explore readiness scenarios when rules are configured—never an official decision.

About FacultyLink

Assessment support, with human review at the center.

FacultyLink is a faculty evaluation and self-assessment platform designed to support document organization, KRA-based scoring, reviewer verification, and rank upgrade simulation. It helps streamline the assessment process while maintaining human review and compliance with applicable evaluation guidelines.

Human review

Reviewers verify submitted documents and results.

Guideline aligned

Built around DBM–CHED Joint Circular No. 01, s. 2026.

Supports, not replaces

Official evaluation remains with authorized committees and the SUC governing board.
